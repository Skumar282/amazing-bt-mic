Privacy Policy

Amazing BT Mic

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Amazing BT Mic (“the App”, “we”, “our” or “us”) handles information when you use the app.

1. Information and Permissions the App Uses

  • Microphone: The App uses your device microphone to capture live audio when you turn the microphone on. Audio is processed for the live microphone feature and routed to the Bluetooth audio output selected by you. The App is not intended to record or upload your live microphone audio.
  • Bluetooth / Nearby Devices: The App uses the relevant Android Bluetooth permissions and system audio-routing features to identify and connect to compatible Bluetooth audio output devices, such as speakers and headsets.
  • Music Files (Optional): If you choose to use the music feature, the App lets you select audio files available on your device. The App accesses the selected audio for playback.
  • Subscription Purchases: If you purchase Amazing BT Mic Pro, the transaction is handled through Google Play. Google may provide purchase and subscription information needed to confirm and manage your entitlement.

2. Audio Processing

Live microphone audio and optional music playback are used to provide the features you activate. Do not use the App in a way that records or transmits another person's voice without their knowledge and any consent required by applicable law.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Google Play as part of app distribution, purchase processing, subscription management and related security checks. Any additional service providers or backend services used by a released version of the App must be identified here before publication.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Audio selected or captured for the App's live features is intended for on-device processing and playback. Purchase records may be retained as needed to validate subscriptions, prevent fraud, provide support and meet legal or accounting obligations. The exact retention period depends on the systems used by the released version of the App.

5. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information processed by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Amazing BT Mic is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

7. Your Choices and Permissions

You can control Android permissions through your device settings. You can turn the microphone off inside the App and choose whether to use optional music playback. Subscription cancellation and payment-related controls are available through Google Play.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
